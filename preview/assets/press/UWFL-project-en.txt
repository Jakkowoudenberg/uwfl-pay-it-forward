UNITED WOOD FLOOR LAYERS - PAY IT FORWARD
Project information

Together, we create one artwork from wooden panels. Every maker brings a personal story and promises to help three people. We pass on more than our craft.

The artwork is intended to travel internationally and grow with new panels and stories. It will never be sold. The aim is to eventually donate it to one or more meaningful public places.

Jakko Woudenberg - Initiator · Dutch Wood Artist
As a father, I keep asking myself: What kind of world are we leaving behind for our children?

Jakko Woudenberg started UWFL and reviews registrations and submissions before publication. Discuss contributions and practical arrangements with him through the contact form.

First planned exhibition: NWFA Expo 2027 · Fort Worth, Texas
27–29 April 2027

For selection for NWFA Expo 2027, the current schedule asks for panel photos by 31 January 2027. The movement has no fixed end date. Read the full schedule and shipping instructions before sending anything.

Registrations, profiles and uploads appear only after Jakko approves them. A new panel or upload from an existing participant is reviewed too.

Everyone is shown on equal terms. The order is shuffled for each new visit.
