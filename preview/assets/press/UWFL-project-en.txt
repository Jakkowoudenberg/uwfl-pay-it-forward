UNITED WOOD FLOOR LAYERS - PAY IT FORWARD
Project information

UWFL invites the worldwide wood flooring industry to create one shared artwork from wooden panels. Makers learn from each other and pass their craft on. Every participant promises to help three people and asks them to pass that help on.

Contributors
Support the project with your time, knowledge or skills, without making a panel. As a contributor, you also help three people and ask them to pass that help on.

The strength of a whole industry
When floor layers, manufacturers, suppliers, schools, trade organisations and trade media work together worldwide, they bring knowledge, materials, transport and reach together. As an industry, we can involve far more people in the project and in helping others.

Knowledge for the next generations
Techniques and experience cross borders. Experienced makers, students and beginners learn together and pass the craft on.

More people helping each other
Every organisation brings its own network. Together, we can bring the invitation to help three people into many more communities.

Our craft as an art form
Through one shared artwork, we show the creativity, skill and care that the whole wood flooring industry can contribute.

From making together to a lasting gift

1. Create and grow regionally
Makers share knowledge and help three people. Local partnerships and presentations help the project grow on each continent and invite others to join.

2. Bring the world together
Our shared aim is to physically bring together all panels from around the world into one artwork. The place and timing will be decided later.

3. Travel, show and involve
Along the journey, we show the art in our craft. We keep inviting people into the project and encouraging them to help others.

4. Eventually donate
After the journey, the aim is to donate the artwork to one or more meaningful public locations. The makers could also lay the floor there together.

The project is open-ended. The place and date for bringing all panels together, the onward journey and the eventual donation are still to be decided.

The artwork will never be sold.

Growing across continents. One shared artwork.
Makers create panels, learn from each other and help three people. Each continent develops its own partnerships and opportunities to show the work, inviting more people into UWFL. Our shared aim is to physically bring together all panels from around the world.

The Americas, including Canada
For now, the NWFA Expo delivery route applies to panels from the Americas, including Canada. A selection of approved panels can be shown in the NWFA booth to introduce the project and invite others to join.

European Union (EU)
EU panels are currently being collected through T&G Wood International. Transport to the US Expo has not been arranged. A possible European presentation in 2027 is in preparation: to show EU panels, introduce the project and involve new participants and partners.

Other countries and regions
You can also take part from other countries. Collection, transport and possible local presentations will be agreed region by region. Wait for personal instructions; there is no general delivery route for these countries yet.

Delivery details are sent to you personally
The shipping address, contact details and specific delivery instructions are sent only by email. Ship only after Jakko approves your panel and your destination and shipment are confirmed. If no route is confirmed, you will first receive an update about the next steps.
