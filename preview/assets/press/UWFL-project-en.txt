UNITED WOOD FLOOR LAYERS - PAY IT FORWARD
Project information

UWFL invites the worldwide wood flooring industry to create one shared artwork from wooden panels. Makers learn from each other and pass their craft on. Every participant promises to help three people and asks them to pass that help on.

The strength of a whole industry
When floor layers, manufacturers, suppliers, schools, trade organisations and trade media work together worldwide, they bring knowledge, materials, transport and reach together. As an industry, we can involve far more people in the project and in helping others.

Knowledge for the next generations
Techniques and experience cross borders. Experienced makers, students and beginners learn together and pass the craft on.

More people helping each other
Every organisation brings its own network. Together, we can bring the invitation to help three people into many more communities.

Our craft as an art form
Through one shared artwork, we show the creativity, skill and care that the whole wood flooring industry can contribute.

From making together to a lasting gift

1. Create together
Every panel carries its own story. Makers, companies, schools and organisations bring together knowledge, materials and help.

2. Travel, show and involve
Along the journey, we show the art in our craft. We keep inviting people into the project and encouraging them to help others.

3. Eventually donate
After the journey, the aim is to donate the artwork to one or more meaningful public locations. The makers could also lay the floor there together.

The artwork will never be sold.
